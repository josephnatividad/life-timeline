# Stories & Privacy-Safe Sharing

## Principle

**The timeline stays private. The user chooses which moments become
Stories.**

Never share a raw record object.

## Pipeline

``` text
Private timeline record
        ↓
Privacy sanitizer
        ↓
User-selected fields
        ↓
Local Story renderer
        ↓
Preview
        ↓
System share sheet
```

No company server is required.

## Privacy defaults

Sensitive fields default OFF.

Examples that should never be suggested for sharing:

-   Passport/document numbers
-   VIN/serial numbers where sensitive
-   Booking references
-   Insurance identifiers
-   Exact home address
-   Private notes

## Story templates

Initial/future templates:

-   Minimal
-   Photo
-   Stats
-   Journey
-   Then & Now
-   Year / Life Wrapped

## Growth role

Stories provide organic distribution without building a social network.

Do not add:

-   Followers
-   Likes
-   Comments
-   Public timeline profiles
-   In-app social feed

Existing social platforms remain the destination.

## Life Wrapped

Generated locally from structured data.

Possible outputs:

-   Trips/countries/cities
-   Milestones
-   Devices/vehicles
-   Career events
-   Years in current home
-   Favorite/selected memories

The user explicitly chooses what appears in a shareable result.
