# Product Vision

## Problem

People create a large digital trail across photos, receipts, documents,
calendars, devices and apps, but years later they often cannot answer
simple questions about their own lives.

The information may exist, but it is fragmented and difficult to
retrieve.

## Vision

Create a private application that turns personal history into a
structured, searchable, understandable timeline.

The timeline should support major life domains such as:

-   Personal milestones
-   Education
-   Career
-   Homes
-   Travel
-   Technology and possessions
-   Vehicles
-   Important documents
-   Financial milestones and major purchases
-   Achievements
-   Family memories where the user chooses to record them

Health/medical record management is deliberately not an MVP domain
because it raises additional sensitivity and regulatory complexity.

## Product loop

``` text
CAPTURE
Photo / document / manual memory
        ↓
REMEMBER
Structured timeline
        ↓
DISCOVER
Search / insights / relationships
        ↓
REFLECT
Anniversaries / milestones / Life Wrapped
        ↓
CELEBRATE
Stories / share cards
        ↓
SHARE
Existing social networks
```

## What this product is not

-   Not a public social network
-   Not a cloud storage company
-   Not a password manager
-   Not a replacement for official document repositories
-   Not an AI chatbot with a timeline attached
-   Not a mandatory subscription SaaS
-   Not a diary requiring daily writing

## Differentiator

The product should be able to truthfully communicate:

> Your timeline is stored on your device and in backups you control. We
> do not operate a cloud copy of your life history.

This promise must be supported technically, not merely through
marketing.

## Experience goal

A new user should experience value quickly by reconstructing something
from their past---not by waiting months to accumulate new events.

The onboarding "wow moment" should invite the user to add an old photo,
receipt or document, extract useful information locally where possible,
and propose a timeline event for confirmation.
