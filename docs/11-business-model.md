# Business Model

## Philosophy

Free should satisfy the core need.

Pro should make maintaining, understanding and celebrating the timeline
dramatically easier.

Never make users feel their own data is being held hostage.

## Free

Recommended permanently free capabilities:

-   Unlimited basic timeline entries
-   Manual entry
-   Basic categories
-   Basic attachments
-   Basic search
-   Reminders/expiry dates
-   PIN/biometric protection
-   Manual backup/restore
-   Basic export
-   Basic Stories
-   A generous taste of Pro intelligence

Do not impose a tiny lifetime entry limit.

## Pro --- one-time purchase

Working pricing hypothesis: approximately USD \$20--40 depending on
market validation.

Potential Pro features:

-   Unlimited supported on-device AI Capture
-   Advanced OCR/document intelligence
-   Advanced local search
-   Life Insights
-   Ask My Life
-   Life Wrapped
-   Advanced Stories/templates
-   Advanced Storage Optimizer
-   Automatic user-owned backup
-   Advanced export
-   Themes/customization

## Free taste of Pro

Prefer usage-based complimentary actions rather than a seven-day timer.

Example:

``` text
50–100 complimentary AI-assisted actions
No expiration
```

The exact allowance is an experiment, not an architectural constant.

Users should experience results before seeing the purchase prompt.

## Conversion principle

Do not sell a feature checklist first. Show realized value:

-   Memories preserved
-   Important expirations discovered
-   Time saved
-   Years organized
-   Insights found
-   Stories generated

When complimentary Pro actions are exhausted, manual core use remains
available.

## Purchase promise

Preferred:

> One purchase. Pro is yours forever.

Avoid promising every future feature forever.

Purchase policy should include maintenance, security, compatibility and
improvements to purchased features while reserving the right to sell:

-   Major future editions
-   Meaningful expansion packs
-   Optional cloud services
-   Physical products
-   New large local AI model packs where justified

## Sustainability

Because timeline storage, search and AI are primarily local, marginal
infrastructure cost per paid user is low.

Long-term revenue can come from:

1.  New Pro customers
2.  Paid major-version upgrades
3.  A small number of meaningful expansions
4.  Optional local AI/model packs
5.  Life Book / printed products
6.  Optional hosted services only if introduced later

## Subscription policy

Do not require subscription for local Pro functionality.

A subscription becomes reasonable only if the company later provides a
real recurring-cost service such as hosted encrypted storage,
multi-device cloud synchronization or cloud AI.

## Advertising

Avoid advertising inside the product. It conflicts with the
trust/privacy positioning.
